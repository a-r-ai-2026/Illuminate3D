# LifeStone Ministries — Content Entry Guide
### Illuminate Bible Series, Volume 1: "God Our Savior"

This guide instructs editors and curriculum specialists on maintaining, extending, and transcribing lessons into `/content/volume-1.json`.

---

### Core Principles
1. **Verbatim Text**: Never paraphrase, summarize, or alter text from the printed Guide Book or Student Workbook. Every line of scripture must follow the New King James Version (NKJV) with proper copyright notices intact.
2. **Age Tracks**:
   - **Explorer Track (1st-2nd Grade)**: Sourced directly from *Student Workbook 1st-2nd, Volume 1*. Simplified narration, clean options, hands-on family activities, and memory verse phrase repetition.
   - **Investigator Track (3rd-6th Grade)**: Sourced directly from *1st-6th Grade Guide Book, Volume 1*. In-depth theological narrative, Scripture Connections, doctrine sidebars, scientific/historical facts, and 12-question review assessments.
3. **5-Day Structure**:
   - Day 1: Discover
   - Day 2: Investigate
   - Day 3: Connect
   - Day 4: Focus
   - Day 5: Illuminate
4. **I Spy Objects**: Every 3D destination features objects mapped directly to the creation days and biblical story events. Objects should be tagged with camera views (`surface` or `underwater` / `detail`) and child-friendly hints.
5. **No Em-Dashes**: Do not use em-dashes anywhere in UI text or JSON fields (use commas, colons, or standard hyphens where needed).
