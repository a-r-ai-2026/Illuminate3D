# Destination Scene Briefs: Volume 1 "God Our Savior"
### LifeStone Ministries — Illuminate Bible Series

Each 3D world destination is designed as an interactive, stylized diorama with warm lighting, gentle atmospheric movement, and key scriptural I Spy objects that correspond directly with the lesson text.

---

### Unit 1: God Creates the World
1. **Lesson 1: Creation Shore** (Active in App)
   - *Setting*: A pristine coastline at dawn where the calm ocean meets golden sand dunes under a sky with both the rising sun and a faint crescent moon. Lush palm fronds sway, mountain ridges anchor the horizon, and playful dolphins leap in the surf. Underwater, a vibrant coral reef flourishes with a gentle sea turtle, luminous jellyfish, seahorses, and starfish.
   - *Cameras*: Surface (dawn beach view) and Underwater (vibrant reef view).
   - *Lighting*: Warm golden key light shifting to crystalline white during Day 4.

2. **Lesson 2: Eden Sanctuary** (Genesis 1:26-31; 2:7-25)
   - *Setting*: The lush, fragrant garden of God with crystal streams converging into four river heads, blooming flowering groves, gentle grazing animals, and an ornate pathway leading to the center of paradise.
   - *Cameras*: Garden Path and River Confluence.

3. **Lesson 3: The Tree of the Knowledge of Good and Evil** (Genesis 3:1-24)
   - *Setting*: An ancient grove within Eden where the prominent tree with radiant fruit stands, marked by soft amber light shifting toward dusky shadow, with the distant garden gate guarded by the cherubim and flaming sword.
   - *Cameras*: Tree Center and Garden Threshold.

4. **Lesson 4: The Valleys of the First Brothers** (Genesis 4:1-16)
   - *Setting*: Rolling agrarian hills with tilled golden grain fields on one side and a gentle shepherd's pasture on the other, featuring stone altars with smoke rising toward heaven.
   - *Cameras*: Pasture Vista and Stone Altars.

### Unit 2: Judgment and the Covenant
5. **Lesson 5: Plains of Gopher Wood** (Genesis 6)
   - *Setting*: An expansive cypress forest clearing with the massive wooden ark under construction, towering scaffolding, timber stacks, and warm lantern-lit workbenches.
6. **Lesson 6: The Boundless Waters** (Genesis 7-8)
   - *Setting*: The ark floating peacefully upon the calm crest of deep blue waters beneath gentle parting rain clouds, with water streams cascading over hills.
7. **Lesson 7: Mount Ararat Heights** (Genesis 8-9)
   - *Setting*: A rugged mountain peak emerging from the mist with the ark resting on dry rock, an altar of thanksgiving, and a vibrant multi-colored rainbow arcing across the sky.
8. **Lesson 8: Plains of Shinar** (Genesis 11)
   - *Setting*: An ancient river plain in Mesopotamia with brick kilns and a spiraling ziggurat tower rising toward the clouds, surrounded by scattered carts and building blocks.

---

### Unit 3: The Patriarchs of Promise
9. **Lesson 9: The Road from Ur** (Genesis 12)
   - *Setting*: A desert caravan path with tents, palm oases, and stone boundary markers under a starry expanse leading into the hills of Canaan.
10. **Lesson 10: The Oaks of Mamre** (Genesis 15, 18)
    - *Setting*: An open encampment under ancient oak trees with woolen tents, a stone altar, and a brilliant starry sky counting the innumerable stars of promise.
11. **Lesson 11: Wells of Beersheba** (Genesis 21)
    - *Setting*: A desert well with stone masonry, tamarisk trees planted in the sand, water troughs, and playful sheep resting in the shade.
12. **Lesson 12: Mount Moriah Ridge** (Genesis 22)
    - *Setting*: A quiet mountain ridge bathed in early sunrise light, with a stone altar, wood bundles, and a ram caught in the nearby thicket.

---

### Unit 4: Jacob and the Twelve Tribes
13. **Lesson 13: The Tents of Isaac** (Genesis 25, 27)
    - *Setting*: Patriarchal desert dwelling with woven goat-hair tents, simmering clay pots over hearth stones, and pastoral grazing lands.
14. **Lesson 14: Bethel Stone Altar** (Genesis 28)
    - *Setting*: A solitary rocky hillside under a midnight blue sky with a stone pillow altar and a shimmering ray of light descending from heaven.
15. **Lesson 15: Dothan Pastures** (Genesis 37)
    - *Setting*: Green hillsides with dry stone wells and grazing flocks under a clear blue sky, with a camel caravan visible on the trade road.
16. **Lesson 16: The Palace of Egypt** (Genesis 41-45)
    - *Setting*: Grand Egyptian limestone courtyard with lotus columns, grain granaries, reflective pools, and royal linen banners.
